## 🎯 Design Concept: Leaderboard-First Conversion Engine

In this layout, **the Pac-Man High Score Leaderboard IS the main hero of the page**.

Arriving students instantly see top peer scores in the BYU CS department. Directly underneath the high scores table sits the **Gamification Hook & Actionable CTA** — an interactive "CLAIM YOUR RANK / INSERT RESUME" upload zone that converts visitors into active users in seconds.

## 🎨 Page Flow Breakdown

1. **Top Hero: Arcade High Score Board**
   - Immediately presents the BYU CS leaderboard upon page load.
   - Tab filters for `ALL MAJORS`, `COMP SCI`, `CYBERSECURITY`, `DATA SCIENCE`.
2. **Actionable Gamification Hook & Upload Zone**
   - High-contrast Arcade CTA: `🕹️ INSERT RESUME TO CLAIM YOUR RANK`.
   - Direct drag-and-drop PDF drop zone.
   - Instant transition from viewer to participant.
3. **Product Overview & 5-Layer Framework**
   - Explains what the 5 layers evaluate (ATS, Core Spec, Impact, 6-Sec Storyline, X-Factor).
4. **RMS Score Benchmarks**
   - Clarifies what `90-100` (Market Ready), `75-89` (Intern Ready), and `<75` (Tourist) mean.
